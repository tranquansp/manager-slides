Why

The miss is entirely e-commerce

Stores hit 103% of plan; e-commerce landed at 85% — a $6.8M shortfall that accounts for the entire gap.

Pharmacy and wholesale were on or above plan. There is no broad demand problem.

Source: channel P&L, July 2026

Root cause

Conversion dropped after the June app update

Conversion −0.9pt

Checkout drop-off rose the week the app 4.2 release shipped.

Traffic flat

Sessions held steady — this is not a demand or spend problem.

AOV −3%

Basket size slipped as bundle recommendations stopped rendering.

Source: web analytics; app release notes 4.2

Options

Two ways to recover the second half

A · Promo push

Discount to buy back volume. Fast, but costs ~1.5pt of margin and does not fix the leak.

B · App fix + retarget

Two-week engineering sprint to restore checkout and bundles, then retarget lapsed carts. Slower, addresses the cause.

Estimates: commercial + engineering
